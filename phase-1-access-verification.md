## Curated relationship constraints

- Choose **A7 or C2**, not both, for the proof-writing foundation.
- Proof foundations precede C1; C1 precedes C3 and A1.
- Choose **A2 or A3** for second-pass elementary enumeration; B1 is backup.
- A5 and A6 validate counts, while B2 belongs before optimization.
- B3, B4, and B5 cover scale and asymptotics after A1.
- B6 is orientation only. C4, followed by C5 as needed, is conditional on
  going beyond $n=5$. D1 is historical only.
